How this works

  1. 01

    You speak

    Describe what you need. Share a transaction or payout reference if you have one.

  2. 02

    The agent checks

    It looks up approved RelayPay help content, or your records once your account is verified.

  3. 03

    You get an answer

    It answers directly, or connects you to a RelayPay specialist.

When a specialist takes over

Some requests are always handled by people: account restrictions, compliance reviews, disputes, refunds, and cancellations. For these, the agent will take your name, email, and preferred callback time, and give you a reference. A RelayPay specialist will then contact you.

Privacy

  • You are speaking with an AI agent, not a person.
  • Calls are recorded and logged for quality and support follow-up.
  • The agent will never read out sensitive account details, and will never ask for your password or card numbers.
